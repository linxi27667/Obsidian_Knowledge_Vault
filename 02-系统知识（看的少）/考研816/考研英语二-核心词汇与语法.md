## 五、五大基本句型

### 5.1 主语 + 谓语 (SV)

> 不及物动词作谓语，无需宾语。

**结构**：`S + Vi`

**例句**：
- The economy **grew** rapidly.（经济快速增长。）
- Prices **fluctuated** throughout the year.（价格全年波动。）
- The experiment **failed**.（实验失败了。）
- Children **develop** at different rates.（儿童以不同速度发育。）

### 5.2 主语 + 谓语 + 宾语 (SVO)

> 及物动词作谓语，需要一个宾语。

**结构**：`S + Vt + O`

**例句**：
- Scientists **discovered** a new species.（科学家发现了一个新物种。）
- The government **implemented** new policies.（政府实施了新政策。）
- Researchers **analyzed** the data carefully.（研究人员仔细分析了数据。）
- Companies **invest** billions in technology.（公司在技术上投资数十亿。）

### 5.3 主语 + 谓语 + 间接宾语 + 直接宾语 (SVOO)

> 双宾语结构，常见动词：give, tell, show, offer, send, teach

**结构**：`S + Vt + IO + DO`

**例句**：
- The teacher **gave** the students a test.（老师给学生做了一次测试。）
- The report **provides** policymakers useful information.（报告为决策者提供了有用信息。）
- She **offered** him a position.（她为他提供了一个职位。）
- The data **tells** us a different story.（数据告诉我们一个不同的故事。）

### 5.4 主语 + 谓语 + 宾语 + 宾语补足语 (SVOC)

> 宾语补足语补充说明宾语的状态或身份。

**结构**：`S + Vt + O + OC`

**例句**：
- The results **made** the researchers confident.（结果使研究人员充满信心。）
- Education **enables** people to improve their lives.（教育使人们能够改善生活。）
- The committee **appointed** her director.（委员会任命她为主任。）
- We **consider** the policy effective.（我们认为该政策有效。）

### 5.5 主语 + 系动词 + 表语 (SVP)

> 系动词连接主语和表语，表语描述主语的性质或状态。

**结构**：`S + Linking V + P`

**常见系动词**：be, seem, appear, become, remain, stay, look, sound, feel, turn, grow, prove

**例句**：
- The situation **remains** unclear.（局势仍不明朗。）
- The results **proved** significant.（结果被证明很重要。）
- The economy **appears** stable.（经济看起来稳定。）
- His argument **sounds** convincing.（他的论点听起来令人信服。）

## 六、三大从句

### 6.1 定语从句 (Attributive Clause)

#### 6.1.1 关系代词

| 关系代词 | 先行词 | 在从句中作 |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **who** | 人 | 主语/宾语 |
| **whom** | 人 | 宾语 |
| **whose** | 人/物 | 定语 |
| **which** | 物 | 主语/宾语 |
| **that** | 人/物 | 主语/宾语 |

**例句**：
- The researcher **who** conducted the study won an award.（进行这项研究的研究员获了奖。）
- The policy **which/that** was implemented last year has shown results.（去年实施的政策已见成效。）
- The student **whose** paper was published received praise.（论文被发表的学生受到了表扬。）

#### 6.1.2 关系副词

| 关系副词 | 先行词 | 含义 |
|----------|--------|------|
| **where** | 地点名词 | 在…地方 |
| **when** | 时间名词 | 在…时候 |
| **why** | reason | …的原因 |

**例句**：
- This is the city **where** I was born.（这是我出生的城市。）
- I remember the day **when** we first met.（我记得我们初次见面的那天。）
- The reason **why** he resigned is unknown.（他辞职的原因不明。）

#### 6.1.3 限制性 vs 非限制性定语从句

| 特征 | 限制性 | 非限制性 |
|------|--------|----------|
| 逗号 | 无 | 有逗号隔开 |
| that | 可用 | 不可用 |
| 省略 | 不可省略 | 可省略（意思完整） |
| 翻译 | "…的" | 补充说明 |

**例句**：
- **限制性**：The book **that** I borrowed was interesting.（我借的那本书很有趣。）
- **非限制性**：The book, **which** I borrowed from the library, was interesting.（这本书很有趣，是我从图书馆借的。）

### 6.2 名词性从句 (Noun Clause)

#### 6.2.1 主语从句

> 从句在句中作主语。

**引导词**：that, whether, what, who, which, when, where, how, why

**例句**：
- **That** the earth is round is a proven fact.（地球是圆的是一个被证明的事实。）
- **Whether** the plan will succeed remains uncertain.（该计划是否会成功仍不确定。）
- **What** he said surprised everyone.（他说的话让所有人惊讶。）
- It is important **that** we act quickly.（重要的是我们要迅速行动。）— it 作形式主语

#### 6.2.2 宾语从句

> 从句在句中作宾语。

**例句**：
- I believe **that** education is the key to success.（我相信教育是成功的关键。）
- The study shows **that** exercise improves health.（研究表明运动改善健康。）
- She asked **whether** the results were reliable.（她问结果是否可靠。）
- We need to determine **what** caused the problem.（我们需要确定是什么导致了这个问题。）

#### 6.2.3 表语从句

> 从句在系动词后作表语。

**例句**：
- The problem is **that** we lack funding.（问题是我们缺乏资金。）
- This is **what** I have been trying to explain.（这就是我一直在试图解释的。）
- The question is **whether** we can afford it.（问题是我们是否负担得起。）

#### 6.2.4 同位语从句

> 从句解释说明前面的抽象名词（fact, idea, news, belief, hope, evidence, suggestion, doubt 等）。

**例句**：
- The fact **that** he resigned surprised everyone.（他辞职的事实让所有人惊讶。）
- There is evidence **that** the treatment is effective.（有证据表明该疗法有效。）
- The news **that** prices would rise caused panic.（物价将上涨的消息引起了恐慌。）

**同位语从句 vs 定语从句的区分**：
- 同位语从句：that 不作成分，从句解释名词内容
- 定语从句：that 作成分（主语/宾语），从句修饰限定名词

### 6.3 状语从句 (Adverbial Clause)

| 类型 | 引导词 | 例句 |
|------|--------|------|
| **时间** | when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as | **When** the results came in, we celebrated. |
| **地点** | where, wherever | **Wherever** you go, you should follow the rules. |
| **原因** | because, since, as, now that, in that | The project failed **because** funding was cut. |
| **结果** | so...that, such...that | The evidence was **so** strong **that** no one could deny it. |
| **目的** | so that, in order that, for fear that | He worked hard **so that** he could pass the exam. |
| **条件** | if, unless, provided that, as long as, on condition that | **If** the data is correct, our hypothesis is confirmed. |
| **让步** | although, though, even though, while, as, no matter what | **Although** the task was difficult, they completed it on time. |
| **方式** | as, as if, as though | Do **as** I say, not **as** I do. |
| **比较** | as...as, not so...as, than, the more...the more | The results were better **than** we expected. |

## 七、非谓语动词

### 7.1 不定式 (Infinitive)

#### 7.1.1 作主语
- **To learn** a language requires patience.（学习一门语言需要耐心。）
- It is essential **to maintain** a balanced diet.（保持均衡饮食是必要的。）

#### 7.1.2 作宾语
- We decided **to postpone** the meeting.（我们决定推迟会议。）
- The company plans **to expand** overseas.（公司计划向海外扩张。）

#### 7.1.3 作定语
- The first person **to arrive** was the manager.（第一个到达的人是经理。）
- We need a place **to conduct** the experiment.（我们需要一个做实验的地方。）

#### 7.1.4 作状语（目的/结果/原因）
- **To improve** the results, we changed the method.（为了改善结果，我们改变了方法。）
- He woke up early **to catch** the train.（他早起是为了赶火车。）
- She was happy **to receive** the award.（她很高兴获得这个奖项。）

#### 7.1.5 作宾语补足语
- The results led us **to believe** the theory.（结果使我们相信了这个理论。）
- I want you **to understand** the importance.（我希望你理解其重要性。）

### 7.2 动名词 (Gerund)

#### 7.2.1 作主语
- **Reading** broadens one's horizons.（阅读开阔视野。）
- **Learning** from mistakes is essential.（从错误中学习是必要的。）

#### 7.2.2 作宾语
- I enjoy **working** in a team.（我喜欢团队合作。）
- She suggested **changing** the approach.（她建议改变方法。）

#### 7.2.3 作介词宾语
- He is good at **solving** problems.（他擅长解决问题。）
- Thank you for **helping** me.（谢谢你帮助我。）

**只能接动名词的动词**：enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, consider, practice, risk, admit, deny, imagine, escape, postpone, resist, appreciate

### 7.3 分词 (Participle)

#### 7.3.1 现在分词 (-ing)

**作定语**：
- The **running** water was cold.（流动的水很冷。）
- The man **standing** at the door is my teacher.（站在门口的那个人是我的老师。）

**作状语**：
- **Walking** along the street, I met an old friend.（沿着街道走时，我遇到了一个老朋友。）
- **Not knowing** what to do, he asked for help.（不知道该怎么办，他寻求了帮助。）

**作宾语补足语**：
- I saw him **crossing** the road.（我看到他正在过马路。）

#### 7.3.2 过去分词 (-ed)

**作定语**：
- The **broken** window was repaired.（破损的窗户被修好了。）
- The data **collected** from the experiment was analyzed.（从实验中收集的数据被分析了。）

**作状语**：
- **Given** more time, we could have done better.（如果给我们更多时间，我们本可以做得更好。）
- **Influenced** by his teacher, he chose to study science.（受老师的影响，他选择了学理科。）

**作宾语补足语**：
- I had my car **repaired**.（我让人修了车。）

### 7.4 非谓语动词的时态与语态

| 形式 | 主动 | 被动 |
|------|------|------|
| 不定式一般式 | to do | to be done |
| 不定式进行式 | to be doing | — |
| 不定式完成式 | to have done | to have been done |
| 动名词/现在分词 | doing | being done |
| 动名词/现在分词完成式 | having done | having been done |
| 过去分词 | — | done |

**例句**：
- He seems **to be working** hard.（他似乎在努力工作。）
- The project is known **to have been completed**.（该项目据信已完成。）
- **Having finished** the report, she went home.（完成报告后，她回家了。）

## 八、特殊句式

### 8.1 倒装句 (Inversion)

#### 8.1.1 全部倒装（谓语在主语前）

**地点/方位副词前置**：
- **Here** comes the bus.（公交车来了。）
- **There** goes the bell.（铃声响了。）
- **On the wall** hangs a painting.（墙上挂着一幅画。）

#### 8.1.2 部分倒装（助动词/情态动词在主语前）

**否定词前置**：
- **Never** have I seen such a beautiful sunset.（我从未见过如此美丽的日落。）
- **Not only** did he apologize, **but** he also compensated.（他不仅道歉了，还做了补偿。）
- **Hardly** had I arrived when it started raining.（我刚到就开始下雨了。）
- **Under no circumstances** should you give up.（在任何情况下你都不应该放弃。）
- **Seldom** does he go out.（他很少出门。）

**only + 状语前置**：
- **Only then** did I realize my mistake.（直到那时我才意识到我的错误。）
- **Only by working hard** can you succeed.（只有努力工作你才能成功。）

**so/such...that 结构**：
- **So** important **is** education **that** we cannot ignore it.（教育如此重要以至于我们不能忽视它。）

**as 引导的让步状语从句**：
- **Try as he might**, he could not open the door.（尽管他尝试了，但他打不开门。）
- **Child as he is**, he knows a lot.（尽管他是个孩子，但他知道很多。）

### 8.2 强调句 (Emphatic Sentence)

#### 8.2.1 It is/was...that/who... 强调句型

**结构**：`It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分`

**例句**：
- **It was** John **who** broke the window.（是约翰打破了窗户。）— 强调主语
- **It was** yesterday **that** I met her.（是昨天我遇见了她。）— 强调时间
- **It was** in the library **that** we first met.（是在图书馆我们第一次见面。）— 强调地点
- **It is** education **that** changes people's lives.（是教育改变了人们的生活。）

**判断方法**：去掉 It is/was 和 that/who，句子仍然完整。

#### 8.2.2 do/does/did 强调谓语

- I **do** believe you.（我真的相信你。）
- She **did** come to the meeting.（她确实来参加了会议。）
- He **does** understand the problem.（他确实理解这个问题。）

### 8.3 虚拟语气 (Subjunctive Mood)

#### 8.3.1 条件句中的虚拟语气

| 类型 | if 从句 | 主句 |
|------|---------|------|
| 与现在事实相反 | did / were | would/could/might + do |
| 与过去事实相反 | had done | would/could/might + have done |
| 与将来事实相反 | did / were to / should do | would/could/might + do |

**例句**：
- **与现在相反**：If I **were** you, I **would** accept the offer.（如果我是你，我会接受这个提议。）
- **与过去相反**：If he **had studied** harder, he **would have passed**.（如果他学习更努力些，他本可以通过的。）
- **与将来相反**：If it **should rain** tomorrow, we **would** cancel the trip.（如果明天下雨，我们会取消旅行。）

#### 8.3.2 含蓄虚拟语气

**without / but for + 名词**（要不是…）：
- **Without** your help, I **would have failed**.（没有你的帮助，我本会失败。）
- **But for** the rain, we **would have gone** out.（要不是下雨，我们本会出去。）

#### 8.3.3 wish 后的虚拟语气

| 时态 | 从句动词形式 |
|------|-------------|
| 与现在相反 | did / were |
| 与过去相反 | had done |
| 与将来相反 | would/could do |

- I wish I **were** taller.（我希望我更高一些。）
- I wish I **had taken** that opportunity.（我希望我当时抓住了那个机会。）
- I wish he **would come** tomorrow.（我希望他明天能来。）

#### 8.3.4 其他虚拟语气用法

**suggest / recommend / demand / insist 等 + that + (should) + do**：
- The doctor suggested that he **(should) take** a rest.（医生建议他休息。）
- It is essential that everyone **(should) be** present.（每个人都到场是必要的。）

**as if / as though**：
- He talks as if he **were** the boss.（他说话的样子好像他是老板似的。）
- She looked as if she **had seen** a ghost.（她看起来好像见了鬼似的。）

**would rather + that + did / had done**：
- I would rather you **stayed** here.（我宁愿你待在这里。）
- I would rather you **had told** me earlier.（我宁愿你早点告诉我。）

## 九、长难句拆解方法

### 9.1 拆解步骤

1. **找主句主谓宾**：先找到主句的主语、谓语和宾语。
2. **识别从句**：找到关系代词/副词、连接词，确定从句类型。
3. **识别非谓语**：找到 -ing、-ed、to do 结构，判断其语法功能。
4. **识别插入语**：两个逗号/破折号之间的内容。
5. **调整语序**：将修饰成分还原到被修饰词前。

### 9.2 十大实例拆解

#### 实例 1：定语从句嵌套

> **原句**：The researchers who conducted the study that was published in the journal Nature found that the results were consistent with previous findings.

**拆解**：
```
主句：The researchers found that...
├── 定语从句1：who conducted the study (修饰 researchers)
│   └── 定语从句2：that was published in the journal Nature (修饰 study)
└── 宾语从句：that the results were consistent with previous findings
```

**译文**：进行这项发表在《自然》杂志上的研究的研究人员发现，结果与之前的发现一致。

#### 实例 2：非谓语 + 从句

> **原句**：Having analyzed the data collected from over 10,000 participants, the team concluded that the treatment was significantly more effective than previously thought.

**拆解**：
```
非谓语状语：Having analyzed the data (完成式，表先发生)
└── 过去分词作定语：collected from over 10,000 participants (修饰 data)
主句：the team concluded that...
└── 宾语从句：that the treatment was significantly more effective than previously thought
    └── 比较状语：than previously thought
```

**译文**：在分析了从一万多名参与者收集的数据后，该团队得出结论：该疗法的效果比之前认为的要显著得多。

#### 实例 3：多重从句

> **原句**：What makes this study particularly interesting is that it demonstrates how a simple intervention, which costs very little, can have a profound impact on people's health.

**拆解**：
```
主语从句：What makes this study particularly interesting
系动词：is
表语从句：that it demonstrates how...
└── 宾语从句：how a simple intervention can have a profound impact
    ├── 非限制性定语从句：which costs very little (修饰 intervention)
    └── 介词短语：on people's health
```

**译文**：使这项研究特别有趣的是，它展示了一种成本极低的简单干预如何能对人们的健康产生深远影响。

#### 实例 4：倒装 + 插入语

> **原句**：Not only did the government implement stricter regulations, but it also, as many experts had suggested, increased funding for environmental protection.

**拆解**：
```
倒装结构：Not only did the government implement stricter regulations
├── 倒装：Not only + 助动词did + 主语government + 谓语implement
并列连词：but
主句：it also increased funding for environmental protection
└── 插入语：as many experts had suggested
```

**译文**：政府不仅实施了更严格的法规，而且还如许多专家所建议的那样，增加了环保资金。

#### 实例 5：虚拟语气 + 被动语态

> **原句**：Had the experiment been conducted under more controlled conditions, it is likely that the results would have been even more conclusive.

**拆解**：
```
省略if的虚拟条件句：Had the experiment been conducted...
= If the experiment had been conducted under more controlled conditions
主句：it is likely that...
└── 主语从句：that the results would have been even more conclusive
    └── 虚拟语气：would have been (与过去事实相反)
```

**译文**：如果实验在更受控的条件下进行，结果可能会更具说服力。

#### 实例 6：长定语 + 同位语

> **原句**：The theory, first proposed by Darwin in his groundbreaking work "On the Origin of Species", that species evolve through natural selection, has been supported by countless studies.

**拆解**：
```
主语：The theory
├── 插入语/过去分词短语：first proposed by Darwin in his groundbreaking work "On the Origin of Species"
├── 同位语从句：that species evolve through natural selection (解释theory的内容)
谓语：has been supported
介词短语：by countless studies
```

**译文**：这一理论——最初由达尔文在其开创性著作《物种起源》中提出，即物种通过自然选择进化——已被无数研究所支持。

#### 实例 7：并列 + 比较结构

> **原句**：While some argue that technology has made our lives easier, others contend that it has, to a greater extent, made us more dependent and less capable of solving problems independently.

**拆解**：
```
让步状语从句：While some argue that...
└── 宾语从句：that technology has made our lives easier
主句：others contend that...
└── 宾语从句：that it has made us more dependent and less capable
    ├── 插入语：to a greater extent
    └── 并列结构：more dependent and less capable of solving problems independently
```

**译文**：虽然一些人认为科技使我们的生活更轻松，但另一些人则认为，它在更大程度上使我们更加依赖他人，更缺乏独立解决问题的能力。

#### 实例 8：复杂介词短语 + 定语从句

> **原句**：In light of the evidence presented at the conference, which showed a clear correlation between diet and chronic disease, the committee recommended that governments take immediate action.

**拆解**：
```
介词短语状语：In light of the evidence
└── 过去分词作定语：presented at the conference (修饰 evidence)
非限制性定语从句：which showed a clear correlation between diet and chronic disease
主句：the committee recommended that...
└── 宾语从句(虚拟)：that governments take immediate action (should省略)
```

**译文**：鉴于会议上提出的证据——这些证据清楚地表明饮食与慢性病之间存在关联——委员会建议各国政府立即采取行动。

#### 实例 9：被动 + 非谓语 + 从句

> **原句**：The data, having been carefully verified by independent analysts, revealed that the company's profits, which had been declining for three consecutive quarters, were beginning to recover.

**拆解**：
```
主语：The data
插入语/非谓语：having been carefully verified by independent analysts (完成被动式)
谓语：revealed
宾语从句：that the company's profits were beginning to recover
└── 非限制性定语从句：which had been declining for three consecutive quarters (修饰 profits)
```

**译文**：经过独立分析师仔细验证的数据显示，该公司的利润——此前已连续三个季度下降——开始回升。

#### 实例 10：强调句 + 从句嵌套

> **原句**：It is precisely because the challenges we face are so complex that we need to adopt a multidisciplinary approach, one that draws on insights from economics, psychology, and sociology.

**拆解**：
```
强调句型：It is precisely because... that...
原因状语从句：because the challenges we face are so complex
└── 定语从句(省略that)：we face (修饰 challenges)
主句：we need to adopt a multidisciplinary approach
同位语：one that draws on insights from economics, psychology, and sociology
└── 定语从句：that draws on insights from... (修饰 one = approach)
```

**译文**：正是因为我们面临的挑战如此复杂，我们才需要采取一种多学科的方法——一种借鉴经济学、心理学和社会学见解的方法。

## 十、近义词辨析 20 组

### 10.1 achieve / attain / accomplish

| 词汇 | 侧重点 | 用法 |
|------|--------|------|
| **achieve** | 通过努力达成目标 | achieve success / achieve a goal |
| **attain** | 达到（正式），常指高度、境界 | attain a standard / attain maturity |
| **accomplish** | 完成任务、使命 | accomplish a mission / accomplish a task |

- She **achieved** great success in her career.（她在事业上取得了巨大成功。）
- He **attained** the rank of professor.（他获得了教授职称。）
- They **accomplished** the mission ahead of schedule.（他们提前完成了任务。）

### 10.2 affect / influence / impact

| 词汇 | 侧重点 | 用法 |
|------|--------|------|
| **affect** | 直接影响（vt.） | affect health / affect performance |
| **influence** | 潜移默化的影响 | influence behavior / influence decisions |
| **impact** | 强烈的冲击性影响 | have an impact on / impact on |

- The weather **affected** the crop yield.（天气影响了作物产量。）
- Parents **influence** their children's values.（父母影响孩子的价值观。）
- The policy had a significant **impact** on the economy.（该政策对经济产生了重大影响。）

### 10.3 allow / permit / enable

| 词汇 | 侧重点 | 用法 |
|------|--------|------|
| **allow** | 允许（较口语） | allow sb to do / allow doing |
| **permit** | 许可（较正式） | permit sb to do / permit doing |
| **enable** | 使能够（强调能力） | enable sb to do |

- The rules **allow** flexible working hours.（规则允许弹性工作时间。）
- The law **permits** citizens to vote at 18.（法律允许公民在18岁投票。）
- Technology **enables** people to work remotely.（科技使人们能够远程工作。）

### 10.4 assure / ensure / insure

| 词汇 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| **assure** | 向…保证 | assure sb that / assure sb of |
| **ensure** | 确保 | ensure that / ensure sth |
| **insure** | 投保 | insure sth against |

- I **assure** you that the product is safe.（我向你保证产品是安全的。）
- We must **ensure** that all data is accurate.（我们必须确保所有数据准确。）
- You should **insure** your house against fire.（你应该给房子投保火险。）

### 10.5 adequate / sufficient / enough

| 词汇 | 侧重点 | 用法 |
|------|--------|------|
| **adequate** | 足够且合格 | adequate for / adequate to |
| **sufficient** | 充分的（正式） | sufficient for / sufficient to |
| **enough** | 足够的（通用） | enough for / enough to do |

- The food was **adequate** for our needs.（食物足够满足我们的需要。）
- There is **sufficient** evidence to proceed.（有充分的证据可以继续。）
- Is there **enough** time to finish?（有足够的时间完成吗？）

### 10.6 decline / decrease / diminish / reduce

| 词汇 | 侧重点 | 用法 |
|------|--------|------|
| **decline** | 逐渐下降（vi.） | decline in / decline by |
| **decrease** | 数量减少（v.） | decrease from...to... |
| **diminish** | 逐渐变小，重要性降低（v.） | diminish in value |
| **reduce** | 人为减少（vt.） | reduce by / reduce to |

- Sales **declined** by 10% last year.（去年销售额下降了10%。）
- The number of accidents **decreased** significantly.（事故数量显著减少。）
- His influence has **diminished** over time.（他的影响力随时间减弱了。）
- We need to **reduce** costs by 20%.（我们需要将成本降低20%。）

### 10.7 discover / invent / create

| 词汇 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| **discover** | 发现已存在的事物 | discover a planet / discover a cure |
| **invent** | 发明新事物 | invent a device / invent a method |
| **create** | 创造，创建 | create a system / create opportunities |

- Scientists **discovered** a new species in the Amazon.（科学家在亚马逊发现了一个新物种。）
- Edison **invented** the light bulb.（爱迪生发明了灯泡。）
- The program **created** thousands of jobs.（该项目创造了数千个就业机会。）

### 10.8 economic / economical

| 词汇 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| **economic** | 经济的，经济学的 | economic growth / economic policy |
| **economical** | 节约的，省钱的 | economical with fuel / economical car |

- The country's **economic** situation is improving.（该国的经济形势正在改善。）
- This car is very **economical** to run.（这辆车很省油。）

### 10.9 effective / efficient / proficient

| 词汇 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| **effective** | 有效的（达到效果） | effective method / effective solution |
| **efficient** | 高效的（不浪费） | efficient system / efficient use of resources |
| **proficient** | 熟练的 | proficient in / proficient at |

- The vaccine is highly **effective**.（该疫苗非常有效。）
- The new system is more **efficient** than the old one.（新系统比旧系统更高效。）
- She is **proficient** in three languages.（她精通三种语言。）

### 10.10 extend / expand / enlarge

| 词汇 | 侧重点 | 用法 |
|------|--------|------|
| **extend** | 延长时间/空间 | extend a deadline / extend a road |
| **expand** | 扩大规模/范围 | expand the business / expand on |
| **enlarge** | 放大尺寸 | enlarge a photo / enlarge the building |

- The deadline was **extended** by two weeks.（截止日期延长了两周。）
